FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION

Opportunity discovery · GTM Engineer

A public event page could establish that a restaurant is hosting a dated music event. It does not establish willingness to join Digginn. GTM would verify the source and apply the documented qualification rules before proposing a handoff.

Partner review · Sales Employee

Sales would check Riga and category fit, inventory and booking evidence. It may record an internal accept, decline, or request for information only after independent review in an eligible guarded run.

Customer care · Customer Satisfaction

If an event time changed, the support routine would classify the issue, check the approved policy, and prepare a response draft for a human to review.

Coordination · Chief of Staff

The Chief of Staff would reconcile run evidence, flag missing partner terms or support policy, and report the next priority. No reconciliation is claimed in this demonstration.

This is a storyboard, not a live business process. It creates no lead, handoff, customer ticket, partner decision, or run record.
